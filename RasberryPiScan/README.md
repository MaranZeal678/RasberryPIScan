# RasberryPIScan

RasberryPIScan is a Raspberry Pi-powered smart surgical lighting concept associated with Stanford Medicine-oriented surgical innovation work.

The scan is designed to act as an intelligent light mounted on a surgery helmet. It follows surgical equipment in real time and automatically shines focused light on the active tools so the surgeon can keep the working area visible without manually repositioning a light.

## Purpose

- Track surgical instruments and equipment during a procedure.
- Aim a helmet-mounted smart light toward the detected equipment.
- Keep the surgical field illuminated as tools move.
- Reduce interruptions caused by manual lighting adjustments.
- Support prototype research and demonstrations for computer vision-assisted surgery.

## Safety Notice

RasberryPIScan is a prototype and research concept only. It is not a certified medical device and should not be used in live surgical care without appropriate validation, safety review, and regulatory approval.
